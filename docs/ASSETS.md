# Assets and art direction

Reference: https://www.voldogfood.com/ — forest green, olive, acid yellow, generous rounded panels, and bold wide typography. No Voldog logo, photography, website background, or commercial font file is redistributed.

Typography: Anybody 800 semi-expanded and DM Sans 400/700, downloaded from Google Fonts and self-hosted. Both are SIL Open Font License fonts; complete notices are in `public/fonts`.

All photographic assets were generated for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool. Originals remain in the generation history; deployed copies are WebP. The product grid was split into four product images and compressed for web delivery. No stock customer photos or invented testimonials are used.

## Hero — `public/images/hero.webp`

Prompt: Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium editorial pet-care website hero photograph, wide 1536x1024. A joyful young golden retriever with fluffy golden fur and a small sage green collar, sitting front-facing, slightly right of center, looking at camera, mouth open with tongue, beautifully detailed real fur, friendly expressive eyes. Seamless muted olive/sage green studio backdrop and floor (#89966b), softly lit with warm daylight, subtle realistic floor shadow. Full body with paws visible toward lower center, ears complete, dog fills about 78% height. Keep generous empty space on left quarter and top 15% for website typography which will be added separately. Art direction: contemporary premium pet brand, playful but sophisticated, tactile photography, soft analog grain. No text, no logos, no props, no watermark.

## Products — `bites.webp`, `fur.webp`, `tummy.webp`, `play.webp`

Prompt: Use case: product-mockup. A perfectly aligned 2 by 2 grid of FOUR separate premium pet product studio photographs, square overall image 1536x1536. Each quadrant is a separate 768x768 photograph; no borders, no montage overlap, generous space around each product. All items photographed from front at slight angle with soft realistic shadows and premium matte materials. Top LEFT: coral-orange resealable treat pouch, label 'goodkind' in bold dark forest green lowercase, smaller 'THE GOOD BITES' and 'SALMON RECIPE', minimal hand drawn fish, pale peach background. Top RIGHT: pale butter-yellow pet shampoo pump bottle, forest green pump, label 'goodkind' and 'FRESH FUR' and 'OAT & ALOE', light sage background. Bottom LEFT: lavender cylindrical jar with lilac lid, label 'goodkind' and 'HAPPY TUMMY' and 'DAILY SUPPLEMENT', pale lavender background. Bottom RIGHT: sculptural muted forest green rubber dog enrichment chew toy shaped like a stacked rounded pebble with opening at top, no lettering, pale warm cream background. Products occupy central 65 percent height of their quadrant, consistent shadows, elevated playful DTC ecommerce branding. Clean crisp print, no extraneous text, no watermark.
